    Our Story

    A family's craft,
    refined for decades.

    Cesar Yunda began his journey in the stone trade in the mid-'90s, spending years mastering every facet of fabrication and installation. In 2013, with personal savings and family support, he opened Stone Bellisimo LLC in Union City, NJ.

    Today, Cesar works alongside his son Cristian, daughter-in-law, and a tight-knit family team. Every project — whether a single vanity or a full kitchen renovation — receives the same personal attention that has earned us the trust of thousands of NJ homeowners.
