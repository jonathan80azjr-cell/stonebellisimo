Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stone Bellisimo LLC ("Stone Bellisimo," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit stonebellisimollc.com (the "Site"), request an estimate, or communicate with us by phone, text message, or email. Please also read our Terms of Use, including the Text Messaging Terms and AI Voice Calls sections.

Who we are

Stone Bellisimo LLC is a countertop fabrication and installation company located at 618 23rd Street, Union City, NJ 07087. You can reach us at 201.553.1919 or cesaryunda@hotmail.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us in an estimate request

When you submit an estimate request on the Site, we collect:

  • Your first and last name, email address, phone number, and ZIP code.
  • Details about your project, such as project type, material, approximate size, timeline, and any message you write to us.
  • How you heard about us.
  • How you would like us to follow up: an AI call as soon as possible, an AI call at a time you request, a text message, or email only. If you request a callback time, we collect that time and its time zone.
  • The time zone reported by your browser, which we use to keep automated calls within permitted calling hours.
  • A record of any consent you give to receive an AI call or text messages, including the version of the consent wording you agreed to and the date and time you submitted it.

Technical and attribution information

When you submit a request, our server also stores, with your request:

  • A one-way hashed (scrambled) version of your IP address, rather than the IP address itself. We use it to help prevent spam and repeated automated submissions and as part of our consent records.
  • Your browser's user agent (a short description of your browser and device type).
  • How you reached the Site, such as campaign tags in the link you followed (for example, UTM parameters), the website that referred you (the referring site's domain only), the page you first landed on, a general device category (for example, mobile or desktop), and a randomly generated visitor and session identifier.

As you browse the Site, our own first-party analytics also records page views and interactions (such as clicks on phone, text, or estimate buttons, gallery views, and page performance) together with the attribution information described above. See "Cookies, local storage, and analytics" below.

AI voice calls

If you choose an AI call and give consent, we collect a written transcript of the call, the call's status (for example, completed, unanswered, or transferred to a team member), and the time of the call. The audio of the call is processed in real time by our service providers to run the conversation and create the transcript. Our system is not set up to save an audio recording of the call. See "AI voice calls and transcription" below.

Text messages

If you choose text messaging and give consent, we collect the messages you send to us, delivery status information for messages we send, and any opt-out request (for example, a reply of STOP).

Email

We collect the content of emails you send us, including replies to our confirmation and follow-up emails, and information about whether our emails were delivered, bounced, or reported as spam.

Information you give us in other ways

If you call our office, visit our showroom, or work with us on a project, we may keep notes and records of those communications and of your project, such as measurements, selections, and scheduling details.

How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Respond to your estimate request and follow up in the way you chose.
  • Send a confirmation email and, where applicable, follow-up emails about your inquiry or project, such as a request for feedback or a review after a completed project.
  • Place an AI call or send a text message about your inquiry, but only if you selected that option and gave consent.
  • Share your request, and any AI call transcript or text replies, with our team so that a team member can follow up.
  • Prepare estimates, schedule measurements and installations, and provide our services.
  • Keep records of consent and opt-out requests.
  • Understand how visitors find and use the Site and which advertising or outreach brings in inquiries.
  • Protect the Site and our forms against spam, abuse, and automated bots, and keep our systems secure.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

AI voice calls and transcription

If you choose an AI call on the estimate form and check the AI-call consent box, our AI voice assistant, "Bella," may call the phone number you provided about your inquiry. Bella is an AI assistant that uses an artificial voice; it is not a person.

  • Every AI call begins with a recorded disclosure: "Hello, this is an automated call from Stone Bellisimo’s AI assistant about the estimate request you submitted. This call is transcribed and shared with our team."
  • The call is placed through Twilio, and the conversation is run by an AI model provided by OpenAI. The audio is sent to these providers in real time so that the assistant can listen and respond and so that a written transcript can be created.
  • The transcript is stored with your estimate request in our database and emailed to our team.
  • If you ask to speak with a person and confirm that you want to be connected, the assistant transfers the call to a team member. The assistant does not transfer the call unless you ask.
  • Automated calls are placed only between 9 a.m. and 8 p.m. in your local time, based on the time zone submitted with your request. We place one automated call per estimate request. If our system cannot confirm whether a call attempt went through, it does not automatically try again.

If you do not want to receive an AI call, choose a different follow-up option on the form. If you tell the assistant during a call that you do not want to be called again, or you contact us using the information below, our team will honor that request.

Text messaging

If you choose text messaging and check the text-message consent box, we may send text messages about your inquiry to the phone number you provided. Our system sends one automated text per estimate request; additional messages may be sent as part of a conversation you continue with us. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Text messages are sent and received through Twilio. For full program details, see the Text Messaging Terms in our Terms of Use.

We do not share your mobile information for marketing. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except with service providers that help us deliver our text messaging service (such as our messaging provider, Twilio), who may use it only to provide that service to us. All other categories of information described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; that information will not be shared with third parties.

How we share information

We share information with the service providers listed below, which process it on our behalf to operate the Site and our follow-up systems. We also share information within our own team as needed to respond to you and provide our services.

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): hosting the Site and our server code, storing estimate requests and related records in our database (Cloud Firestore), and running our AI voice-call service.
  • Google (reCAPTCHA Enterprise through Firebase App Check): helping to protect our forms and analytics from bots and abuse. reCAPTCHA collects information about your device and how you interact with the page and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Google (Maps and Fonts): displaying an embedded map of our location and loading the typefaces used on the Site. When these load, your browser connects to Google's servers.
  • Google Analytics: when enabled on the Site, measuring how visitors use the Site.
  • Cloudflare: website analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) that measures page views and site performance.
  • Twilio: placing AI voice calls, providing the recorded disclosure at the start of each call, transferring calls to our team on request, and sending and receiving text messages.
  • OpenAI: running the AI voice assistant's conversation and transcribing AI calls.
  • Postmark: sending our confirmation, follow-up, and team notification emails and processing email replies.

We may also disclose information if we believe it is required by law, subpoena, or other legal process; to protect the rights, property, or safety of Stone Bellisimo, our customers, or others; or in connection with a sale, merger, or transfer of all or part of our business, in which case this policy will continue to apply to information transferred.

We do not sell your personal information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Cookies, local storage, and analytics

The Site uses browser storage and similar technologies:

  • First-party analytics. Our own analytics stores a randomly generated visitor identifier, information about how you first arrived at the Site (landing page, referring site, and campaign tags), and a short queue of pending analytics events in your browser's local storage. It stores a session identifier, which expires after 30 minutes of inactivity, in session storage. This information is sent to our own server, where visitor and session identifiers are stored in hashed form.
  • Bot protection. Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise, used through Firebase App Check, may set cookies or use similar technologies to tell people apart from automated bots.
  • Google Analytics. When Google Analytics is enabled on the Site, it uses cookies to measure visits. You can learn how Google uses this information at How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and you can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics. Cloudflare describes its Web Analytics as not using cookies to track visitors.
  • Google Maps. The embedded map is provided by Google and may set its own cookies.

You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings, and you can use browser extensions that block analytics scripts. If you block these technologies, parts of the Site, such as the estimate form's bot protection, may not work as intended. The Site does not currently respond differently to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

The Site contains links to our profiles on social media and other websites, such as Instagram, Facebook, X, Pinterest, Houzz, and Google. Those sites are operated by others and have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep information

We keep estimate requests, consent and opt-out records, AI call transcripts, text and email communications, and project records for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, provide our services, keep accurate business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements. When we no longer need information for these purposes, we delete it or keep it in a form that no longer identifies you. Our service providers may keep information for limited periods under their own retention practices.

Your choices

  • Text messages. Reply STOP to any text from us to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.
  • AI calls. AI calls happen only if you choose that option and give consent. To stop AI calls, tell the assistant during the call that you do not want to be called again, or contact us at 201.553.1919 or cesaryunda@hotmail.com.
  • Email. You can ask us to stop sending follow-up or review-request emails by replying to one of our emails or contacting us.
  • Access, correction, and deletion. You can ask to see the personal information we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it by emailing cesaryunda@hotmail.com or calling 201.553.1919. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and we may keep certain information when we need it for legal or business record-keeping purposes, including a record of your opt-out so that we can honor it.
  • Analytics. See "Cookies, local storage, and analytics" above.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law. To make a request, contact us using the information below.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures designed to protect the information we collect. For example, access to estimate requests and call transcripts is limited to authorized team members, connections to the Site are encrypted, IP addresses are stored in hashed form, and our systems verify that call and text notifications claiming to come from Twilio are authentic before processing them. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information.

Children

The Site and our services are intended for adults. They are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above and post the updated policy on this page. If we make a material change to how we use information collected under a previous version of this policy, we will provide additional notice where appropriate.
