Owner Highlight

Meet Cesar
Yunda.

Cesar began his journey in the stone trade in the mid-'90s, learning every part of the craft from fabrication to installation. After years of building his skill and reputation, he opened Stone Bellisimo LLC in Union City in 2013 with personal savings and the support of his family.

Today, Cesar remains closely involved in the work. He leads with the same care that shaped the company from the beginning: listen first, measure carefully, cut precisely, and treat every home like it matters.

"A beautiful countertop starts with trust, patience, and craftsmanship you can stand behind."
