Our Story

A family's craft,
refined for decades.

Cesar Yunda began his journey in the stone trade in the mid-'90s, spending years perfecting every facet of fabrication and installation. In 2013, with savings and the support of his family, he opened Stone Bellisimo LLC right here in Union City, NJ.

Today, Cesar works alongside his son Cristian, daughter-in-law, and a tight-knit family team. Every project — whether it's a single vanity or a full kitchen renovation — receives the same personal attention and pride that has earned us the trust of thousands of homeowners across New Jersey.
