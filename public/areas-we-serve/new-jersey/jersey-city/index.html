Materials We Fabricate

Quartz, granite, marble, quartzite, and porcelain.

Quartz

The top pick for Jersey City condos — non-porous, low-maintenance, and available in consistent modern whites, greys, and veined looks. Great for busy kitchens and rental-ready units.

Granite & Quartzite

Natural stone with unique movement and serious durability. Ideal for homeowners who want a one-of-a-kind slab and heat resistance for real cooking.

Marble & Porcelain

Classic marble for vanities and feature areas, plus large-format porcelain for ultra-thin, stain-resistant surfaces and full-height backsplashes.

Jersey City FAQs

Questions Jersey City homeowners ask us most.

Do you install in Jersey City high-rise and condo buildings?

Yes. We regularly install in condos and high-rises in Newport, Exchange Place, Paulus Hook, and Journal Square. We provide a Certificate of Insurance (COI) for your building management, coordinate freight-elevator reservations, and protect common areas during delivery and installation.

How far is Stone Bellisimo from Jersey City?

Our shop and showroom is at 618 23rd Street in Union City, NJ — only a few miles from Jersey City. Being local means faster templating, quicker installation scheduling, and easy in-person slab selection.

How long does a Jersey City countertop project take?

After you approve the estimate and material, we template (measure) your space, then typically fabricate and install within about one to two weeks. Timing depends on material availability and your building's access rules.

Which countertop material is best for a Jersey City condo kitchen?

Quartz is the most popular choice for Jersey City condos because it's non-porous, low-maintenance, and available in consistent modern colors. Granite and quartzite suit homeowners who want natural stone, and marble is available for vanities and feature areas. We'll help you choose based on use, budget, and design.
