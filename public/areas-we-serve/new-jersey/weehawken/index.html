Weehawken FAQs

Questions Weehawken homeowners ask us most.

Do you work in Port Imperial and Weehawken waterfront condo buildings?

Yes. We regularly install in Weehawken's waterfront towers and provide a Certificate of Insurance (COI) for building management, reserve the freight elevator, and protect common areas throughout delivery and installation.

Do you carry exotic marble and quartzite slabs?

Yes — for Weehawken projects we source premium and exotic natural stone, including dramatic quartzite and marble, and can arrange viewing of full slabs before fabrication.

Can you book-match slabs for a feature island or backsplash?

Absolutely. Book-matching mirrors the veining across two slabs for a striking, continuous look — a favorite for waterfront kitchens with open sightlines.

How far is your shop from Weehawken?

Our shop and showroom is minutes away at 618 23rd Street in Union City, so templating, slab selection, and installation scheduling are all fast and local.
