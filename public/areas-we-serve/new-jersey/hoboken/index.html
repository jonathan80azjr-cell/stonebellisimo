Brownstones and Towers

Built for narrow walk-ups and waterfront high-rises.

Hoboken installs come with two very different challenges — and we're set up for both:

Brownstone walk-ups: narrow turning staircases with no elevator are routine here. Our team is experienced hand-carrying slabs up tight stairwells, and when a single slab won't make the turn we plan seams so the finished top still looks clean.

Waterfront condo towers: for managed buildings like Maxwell Place and the Toll Brothers towers we provide a Certificate of Insurance (COI) naming your building, reserve the freight elevator, and protect common areas.

Compact galley kitchens: we template precisely and recommend light, low-seam quartz layouts that make small Hoboken kitchens feel larger and brighter.

Hoboken FAQs

Questions Hoboken homeowners ask us most.

Do you install in Hoboken brownstones and walk-ups without an elevator?

Yes — it's one of the most common Hoboken situations we handle. Our team is experienced carrying slabs up narrow, turning staircases, and when a full slab can't make the turn we plan discreet seams so the finished countertop still looks seamless.

Do you provide a Certificate of Insurance for my Hoboken condo building?

Yes. For waterfront and managed buildings like Maxwell Place we supply a COI naming your building and management company, and we reserve the freight elevator so your install is approved without delays.

What countertop is best for a small Hoboken galley kitchen?

Quartz in a light color with minimal seams is usually the best fit — it's non-porous, low-maintenance, and keeps a compact kitchen feeling bright and open.

How far is your shop from Hoboken?

Our shop and showroom is at 618 23rd Street in Union City — just minutes from Hoboken, which means fast templating and flexible install scheduling.
