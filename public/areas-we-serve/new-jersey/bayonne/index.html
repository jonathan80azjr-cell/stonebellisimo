Bayonne FAQs

Questions Bayonne homeowners ask us most.

Do you build full kitchen islands and waterfall edges in Bayonne homes?

Yes. Bayonne's single-family kitchens have the space for it, and we fabricate mitered waterfall islands and large single-slab tops in our own shop for a clean, seamless look.

Granite or quartz for a Bayonne family kitchen?

Both are excellent. Quartz is non-porous and virtually maintenance-free; granite and quartzite are natural stone with unmatched heat resistance and unique veining. We'll walk you through samples so you can decide based on how you actually use your kitchen.

Do you offer free in-home measurements in Bayonne?

Yes — after you choose a direction we template (measure) your space at no cost, then give you a clear, itemized estimate before any work begins.

How soon can you install in Bayonne?

Once your material is selected and the template is done, we typically fabricate and install within about one to two weeks.
